Data Privacy Policy & Notice

Republic Act No. 10173 Compliant

Talisay Senior High School is committed to respecting and protecting the personal privacy of all learners, parents, guardians, faculty, and administrative staff. In compliance with Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) and the guidelines of the Department of Education (DepEd), this policy explains how information is collected, processed, and safeguarded within the Mentorae Student Information System (SIS).

1. Scope of Information Collected

The portal collects and processes only the information necessary to fulfill educational, academic, and administrative mandates:

  • Learner Records: Full legal name, Learner Reference Number (LRN), Senior High School Track and Strand, Section Assignment, attendance logs, quarterly assessment marks, and official School ID photograph.
  • Parent / Guardian Information: Full name, verified mobile contact number, active email address, and relationship to the learner for emergency notices and attendance updates.
  • Faculty & Staff Records: Employee ID, DepEd institutional email, subject teaching loads, classroom schedule assignments, and digital validation signatures.
2. Lawful Purposes for Data Processing
Data processed within Mentorae SIS is used strictly for legitimate educational and institutional activities, including:
  • Recording daily morning routine and classroom attendance via secure QR code validation.
  • Computation of quarterly grades and generation of official DepEd Form 137 (Permanent Record) and Form 138 (Report Card).
  • Dispatching automated SMS and email notifications to parents regarding arrival, late check-in, or absence.
  • Monitoring learner progress and providing early intervention guidance for academic support.
3. Data Security & Access Controls

Mentorae SIS utilizes comprehensive organizational, physical, and technical security measures:

  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Learners and parents access only their own individual records. Teachers access only their assigned classes. Administrative overrides are restricted to authorized school personnel.
  • Cryptographic Protection: User passwords are encrypted using high-work-factor bcrypt hashing. All communications between your device and the portal are secured with TLS/HTTPS encryption.
  • Audit Logging: System authentication, attendance confirmations, and grade submissions are timestamped and logged for administrative accountability.

Acceptable Use Policy (AUP)

All authorized users of the Mentorae Portal are expected to conduct themselves with integrity, responsibility, and adherence to the DepEd Child Protection Policy (DO 40, s. 2012) and the Code of Conduct for Public School Teachers and Learners.

1. User Account Responsibilities
  • Each user is assigned unique credentials. Passwords must remain confidential and must never be shared with classmates, peers, or unauthorized persons.
  • Users must immediately notify the school ICT Coordinator or Class Adviser if they suspect unauthorized access or compromised credentials.
  • Users must log out of their session when using shared school computer laboratories, library terminals, or public devices.
2. Prohibited System Activities
The following actions are strictly prohibited and constitute grounds for administrative and disciplinary action:
  • Attempting to probe, scan, bypass, or test vulnerabilities of the portal infrastructure or network.
  • Attempting to access, modify, or falsify academic grades, attendance timestamps, or administrative configurations.
  • Uploading malicious software, scripts, unauthorized files, or offensive material into lesson repositories or communication boxes.
  • Using another individual's identity, LRN, or QR code to misrepresent school attendance or academic submissions.
3. Disciplinary Sanctions

Violations of this Acceptable Use Policy will be subjected to the school's disciplinary guidelines under the supervision of the Prefect of Discipline and the Office of the Principal, and may result in immediate account revocation, academic sanctions, or legal referral under applicable Philippine laws.

Digital Attendance & QR Code Protocol

To ensure student safety, punctuality, and accurate DepEd reporting, Talisay Senior High School enforces automated QR code attendance scanning at classroom and designated campus entry points.

1. Non-Transferability of QR Credentials

A student's QR code is uniquely linked to their legal LRN and school identity. Proxy scanning (scanning another student's QR code on their behalf, or taking a screenshot of a friend's code to scan for them) is strictly classified as dishonesty and forgery under the Student Handbook.

3. Parent Notification Guarantee

Once attendance is scanned and confirmed by the designated teacher or adviser, the system automatically dispatches real-time records to the Parent Portal and linked notification channels, providing parents with direct transparency over their child's safety and attendance.

Data Subject Rights & Data Protection Officer

Under Section 16 of the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), every learner, parent, and faculty member is recognized as a Data Subject and possesses the following enforceable statutory rights:

1. Right to Be Informed
You have the right to know whether your personal data is being collected, stored, or processed by the school.
2. Right to Access
You have the right to inspect and review your academic grades, attendance logs, and registered parent information.
3. Right to Rectification
You have the right to request correction of inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated information in your profile.
4. Right to Object & Lodge Complaints
You may submit concerns to the School Data Protection Officer or the National Privacy Commission (NPC).
